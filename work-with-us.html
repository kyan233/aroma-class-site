Work with us

We are looking for passionate, hardworking and friendly people to join our Italian deli kitchen and wine bar.

Whether you are a kitchen assistant, a member of bar staff, a waiter or waitress, or simply someone with a fantastic work ethic and a love for hospitality, we would love to hear from you. All experience levels considered.

What we are looking for

  • A friendly and positive attitude
  • A strong work ethic
  • Flexible and reliable
  • Team players who thrive in a fast-paced environment
  • A passion for food, wine and the customer experience

If you would like to be part of a new concept and grow with us from the beginning, email us with a few lines about yourself and your CV if you have one.
