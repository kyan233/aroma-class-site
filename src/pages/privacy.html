Privacy

What we do with the details you give us. Short version: we use them to look after your booking, and nothing else.

Who we are

Aroma Class, Dukes Court, Duke Street, Woking, Surrey GU21 5BH. Contact us about your data at info@aromaclassitalian.com.

What we collect

When you book a table: your name, email, phone number, the number of guests, the date and time, and any notes you add. When you join our emails: your email address.

Why we use it

To hold your table, confirm it, and contact you if something changes. That is what you asked us to do, which is our lawful basis. If you join our emails, we send you our specials, and you can stop them at any time by replying to any email.

Where it is kept

Bookings are stored in a private Google Calendar and sent to us by email. The owner may also get a WhatsApp alert with the booking details. We never sell your details or pass them to anyone for marketing.

How long we keep it

Booking details are deleted within 12 months of your visit. Email sign-ups stay until you ask us to remove you.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you by emailing us. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
