Straight from Italy, made to order.

Welcome to Aroma Class, the Italian deli and wine bar at Dukes Court. Pizzas, ciabattas, salads, pasta and fresh fish, all made to order, alongside a shelf of produce imported directly from Italy and a wine list built for pairing.

We are in central Woking, a five minute stroll from the station and the Peacocks Centre. Come for breakfast, gather with friends for lunch, or join us in the evening for a glass of something Italian.

From the counter and the oven

Stone-baked pizza

12 inch house dough, thin crust, ten classics plus a weekly special or two.

Breakfast, all day

Ciabattas, smashed avocado on sourdough, pastries from the counter.

Coffee by day, spritz by night

Kimbo espresso from 7:30am. Aperol, Limoncello and Hugo spritz later on.
